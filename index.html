Our Foundation

Two ideas hold up
everything we do.

Vision

A leading school wherein learners are developed to fear God, and excel in academics and character, who will become future leaders in following Biblical principles that will uplift the falling world.

Mission

The heart of education is the education of the heart. SLA will train and mold children to love and honor God and country and become socially responsible through high quality education.

Moments at SLA

A community
that lights the way.

Fifteen years of recognitions, championships, fellowships, and quiet ordinary days. SLA isn't just a school — it's a community of students, teachers, parents, and alumni walking the same path.
