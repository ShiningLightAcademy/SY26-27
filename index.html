Our Foundation

Two ideas hold up
everything we do.

01 — Vision

A leading school where learners become future leaders.

A leading school wherein learners are developed to fear God, and excel in academics and character — those who will become future leaders following Biblical principles that uplift the world.

02 — Mission

The heart of education is the education of the heart.

SLA trains and molds children to love and honor God and country, and to become socially responsible through high-quality, holistic education.

Moments at SLA

A community
that lights the way.

Fifteen years of recognitions, championships, fellowships, and quiet ordinary days. SLA isn't just a school — it's a community of students, teachers, parents, and alumni walking the same path. Every photo tells a piece of the same story: that with God's grace, every SLAion finds their place to shine.

From Olympiad medals to outreach Sundays, sports days to graduation tears — this is what fifteen years of "making a difference" actually looks like.
